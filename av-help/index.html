Safety first

Keep it simple.

Use only normal remotes, apps and accessible controls. Leave mounted equipment and wiring in place. Skip a check if you’re unsure — these checks don’t diagnose hardware faults.

If equipment is hot, wet, damaged or smells unusual, stop using it and get qualified help. Don’t open equipment, factory-reset devices or change alarm, camera or network security settings.
