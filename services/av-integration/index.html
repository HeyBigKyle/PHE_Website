FAQ

A few useful answers.

Can you use equipment I already own?

Often, yes. Share model numbers and photos so we can check compatibility and recommend what to keep or upgrade.

What is included in the installation?

Your quote lists the equipment, mounting, wiring, connections and setup included. Wall repair, painting, electrical work and subscriptions are confirmed separately where needed.

What should I send to an AV installer for a quote?

Start with room photos, your city, existing equipment and what you want to improve. For a build or remodel, include floor plans and your timeline.

Can I get support after installation?

Ask about follow-up service and warranty terms for your project. PHE Membership offers ongoing technology care and member service savings.
