Plan first

The details that shape your installation.

Heat · Height · Support · Connections — your equipment and room determine suitability.

  1. 01

    Heat & manufacturer requirements

    Review the fireplace type, mantel, heat at the proposed location and both manufacturers’ instructions. There is no universal clearance for every fireplace and TV.

  2. 02

    Comfort from your seat

    A screen can look balanced on a wall and still feel too high. Consider your normal seating position and alternatives elsewhere in the room.

  3. 03

    Mount movement & support

    A pull-down mount may help in suitable installations. Its load, movement path and clearances need review; movement does not remove heat restrictions.

  4. 04

    Power & signal routes

    A chimney, firebox or masonry wall can limit access. Agree on cable routes and any electrical, wall or finish work before installation.

FAQ

A few useful answers.

How high should a TV sit above a fireplace?

There is no single measurement. Fireplace and TV requirements, actual heat conditions and viewing comfort all matter.

Can every fireplace use a pull-down TV mount?

No. The wall support, TV, mount movement and fireplace requirements determine suitability.

Can you mount through fireplace tile?

Tile adds a separate material and support assessment. We review the surface and fireplace conditions together.

Can the wires be hidden?

Access around the chimney and firebox can limit concealed routes. We review practical signal and power options before confirming the scope.
