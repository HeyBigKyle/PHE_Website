FAQ

Conference room AV questions.

Can the room support Teams or Zoom?

We review your platform and selected equipment. Account, licensing, device compatibility and IT requirements are confirmed for the intended workflow.

Can visitors use their own laptops?

That can be part of the design. Discuss device types, connectors and guest-network policies before choosing the connection method.

Will new microphones fix every echo problem?

No. Room acoustics, speaker placement, processing and microphone coverage all affect calls. The cause should be assessed before selecting equipment.

Do you show staff how to use the room?

An agreed walkthrough covers the installed workflow. Additional training, support and documentation should be listed in the quote.
