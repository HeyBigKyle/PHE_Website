FAQ

Control systems questions.

Can you simplify a system with several remotes?

We review the TV, receiver, sources and existing control equipment to identify supported ways to simplify operation.

Will Nice ELAN control every device?

No platform supports every product. We check model compatibility and explain the available functions before agreeing the scope.

How is this different from home automation?

This service focuses on interfaces and AV programming. Home automation also covers routines involving supported lighting, shades, climate and other devices.

What should I send for a quote?

Equipment model numbers, photos of existing controls and two or three activities you want to make easier.
