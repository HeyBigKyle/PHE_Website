FAQ

In-ceiling & in-wall speakers questions.

Can existing ceiling speakers work with Sonos?

Possibly, through suitable equipment such as a compatible amplifier. Speaker requirements, wiring and the intended layout must be checked first.

How many ceiling speakers does a room need?

It depends on the room, listening purpose, speaker design and coverage goals. A fixed spacing rule is not suitable for every installation.

Can you install speakers after the home is finished?

Often, but access and construction determine the available cable routes and locations. Any wall or ceiling repairs are agreed separately.

Do more speakers mean more independent rooms?

No. Independent sources and volume depend on the amplifier channels, system controls and zone design, not simply speaker quantity.
