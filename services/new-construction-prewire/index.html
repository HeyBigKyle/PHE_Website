Before drywall

Give every connection a destination.

  1. 01

    Rooms & listening zones

    Mark TVs, speakers and the spaces that need independent music. Include furniture and viewing positions in the plan, rather than only counting devices.

  2. 02

    Equipment location

    Choose an accessible location for amplifiers, network equipment and sources. Review power, space and ventilation using the selected equipment's requirements.

  3. 03

    Cable routes & future access

    Plan appropriate speaker, signal and network runs for the actual routes. Discuss spare pathways where useful; no wiring plan guarantees compatibility with every future device.

  4. 04

    Coordination & documentation

    Agree who installs, labels and checks each run before it is concealed. Identify electrical work, construction tasks and final equipment setup separately in the scope.

FAQ

New construction & remodel pre-wire questions.

When should AV pre-wiring be planned?

Before walls and ceilings close, with time to coordinate routes and equipment locations. Contact us early in design or remodeling.

Does pre-wiring include the final equipment?

Only when listed in your quote. Cable installation, termination, testing, equipment and programming can be separate stages.

Can you work with my builder or electrician?

We can coordinate the agreed AV scope and timing. Responsibilities, access and required trade work should be clear before installation starts.

What should I keep after the work?

Ask for the agreed cable labels, endpoint notes and any available route documentation. Confirm testing and documentation deliverables in your quote.
