The details

A finished look starts behind the screen.

  1. 01

    Model & mount fit

    Share the full TV model and supplied mount details. Mounting hardware, connections and installation requirements vary by model and size.

  2. 02

    Height & alignment

    Review the wall, nearby artwork, furniture and seated viewing position. The best visual alignment should also make everyday watching comfortable.

  3. 03

    Connection equipment

    Plan where any separate connection box and source devices will sit. Check cable type, route, length and manufacturer requirements before promising concealed wiring.

  4. 04

    Sound & finish details

    Include the soundbar, Sonos setup or other audio early. Tile, masonry and fireplace walls need their own surface, support or heat review.

FAQ

Frame TV installation questions.

Will every Frame TV sit the same distance from the wall?

No. The model, mount, wall condition and connections influence the finished fit. We review the equipment before confirming expectations.

Can the connection box go inside a wall?

Only with an appropriate solution that meets the equipment and installation requirements. Ventilation, access and power need separate consideration.

Can you add Sonos or a soundbar?

Yes, with compatible equipment. We plan its position, connection and control alongside the TV.

Use the TV Planner for a starting estimate. PHE confirms model fit, wall conditions, cable routing and the final price before booking.
