FAQ

Whole-home audio questions.

Can different rooms play different music?

Yes, with a system designed for independent listening zones. The equipment and music sources determine what is supported.

Do I need in-ceiling speakers?

No. Architectural speakers, freestanding speakers and connected products such as Sonos can suit different spaces.

Can existing ceiling speakers be reused?

Possibly. We review speaker models, wiring condition and amplifier requirements before recommending what to keep.

When should I plan wiring for a remodel?

Before walls and ceilings are closed. Share floor plans and your contractor’s schedule so cable routes and speaker positions can be coordinated.
