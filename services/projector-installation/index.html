FAQ

Projector & screen installation questions.

Should I choose a projector or a large TV?

Room light, image size, seating and budget guide the choice. We can compare both approaches for your space.

Can a projector go anywhere on the ceiling?

No. Lens requirements, image size, mounting support and access determine suitable positions. We check the exact model.

Can you install a motorized screen?

Discuss the screen model, available structure, power and controls. Installation requirements and any supporting trade work must be reviewed.

Can I reuse my existing receiver?

Possibly. The sources, video formats and audio setup determine compatibility. Share model numbers for review.
