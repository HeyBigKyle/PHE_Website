FAQ

Sonos installation questions.

Can Sonos handle both TV and music?

Compatible Sonos products can support both. The soundbar, TV connections and speaker models determine the available setup.

Can I keep my existing speakers?

Possibly. Sonos Amp can power suitable passive speakers; Sonos Port can connect compatible audio equipment. We check the models and wiring first.

Can you add a subwoofer and rear speakers?

We can plan compatible Sonos home theater combinations. Send the model numbers so we can confirm support and placement.

What should I send for a quote?

Your Sonos and TV models, room photos and the areas where you want music. Let us know about any Wi-Fi or playback issues.
