Cable management

Want the wires out of sight?

Start with the wall and the route, whether you are hiding TV cables or getting speaker wires off the floor. We can discuss organizing visible cables, a surface raceway or suitable in-wall low-voltage wiring where access allows.

Ordinary power cords do not belong inside walls. Any needed electrical work, wall repair or finish work is identified separately in your quote.

FAQ

Home theater questions.

Do I need a dedicated theater room?

No. A living room can work well. Seating, light, speaker locations and wiring access help determine the right setup.

Can you install a 7.1 surround sound system?

Yes, where the space and equipment support it. A 7.1 system adds rear surrounds to a 5.1 layout. We review placement before recommending the system.

Should I choose a projector or a TV?

Tell us your viewing distance, available light and preferred screen size. We’ll discuss both options and what fits your budget.

What should I send for a quote?

Room photos, dimensions, seating locations and equipment model numbers. Include your city and any construction timeline.
