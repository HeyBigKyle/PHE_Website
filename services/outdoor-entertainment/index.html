FAQ

Outdoor audio & entertainment questions.

Can I put a regular indoor TV outside?

Equipment needs to suit the location. Arizona heat, sunlight, weather exposure and manufacturer requirements guide the recommendation.

Can outdoor speakers play the same music as inside?

Compatible systems can support shared or independent listening zones. Tell us how you want the spaces to work together.

Where should outdoor speakers go?

We start with seating and gathering areas, then review coverage and cable access. Good placement helps avoid relying on excessive volume.

What should I send for a quote?

Wide photos of the patio and yard, covered and exposed areas, existing outlets and the places where you want sound or a TV.
