FAQ

Motorized shades questions.

Will motorized shades work with my smart home system?

Compatibility is model-specific. We review the motor, control platform and functions you want before including integration in the quote.

Do shades have to be hardwired?

No. Some systems offer battery or plug-in power. The chosen product, window access and maintenance preferences guide the recommendation.

Can existing shades be motorized?

Sometimes, but the existing treatment and compatible hardware need assessment. New shades may be the more suitable option.

Will every shade fully black out a room?

Fabric, mounting and edge gaps affect light control. Discuss the intended result and available options before selecting the treatment.
