FAQ

Ethernet & structured cabling questions.

Will Ethernet make my internet plan faster?

Wiring can improve supported local connections, but it does not increase the speed supplied by your internet provider or resolve every network issue.

Is Cat6 installation right for my network?

The intended equipment, route and project requirements guide the specification. Confirm the proposed cable and expected network capability in your quote.

Can existing network cable be reused?

Possibly. Cable condition, termination and the intended use should be checked before relying on it.

Are testing and labeling included?

Confirm the agreed checks, labels and documentation in the quote. Basic connection checks and formal certification are different deliverables.
