AV Project Planning

Planning your AV installation.

Five details to prepare.

Call (877) 743-8823 Request a quote
★★★★★ 5.0 · 116 Google reviews By PHE Team · Published October 8, 2026

Before requesting a home theater, Sonos or audio video installation quote, gather the details that explain your room and goals. These five steps help PHE discuss equipment compatibility, cable routes and installation scope with you.

1. Describe the experience

Start with what you want to improve: movie nights, television sound, music in several rooms, outdoor entertaining, or simpler controls. Equipment decisions should support that outcome.

2. Show the room

Take wide photos of the space and closer photos of walls, connections, and equipment locations. Note seating positions, available light, and access for wiring.

3. List existing equipment

Model numbers for TVs, Sonos products, speakers, receivers, controls, and network equipment help clarify compatibility and what can be reused.

4. Share a budget range

Installation conditions, equipment selection, and integration scope influence cost. A range helps prioritize the parts of the experience that matter most to you.

5. Explain the timing

Include move-in dates, construction stages, or remodel milestones. Coordinate technology planning with your contractor while cable routes and equipment locations remain accessible. AV integration Home theater Sonos installation
