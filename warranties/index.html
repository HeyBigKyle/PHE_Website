Before you enroll

A clear plan before you enroll.

Request the applicable CPS plan documents and review the coverage period, exclusions, claim limits, any fees and how service is arranged. Mechanical and electrical failure coverage depends on the selected plan; accidental damage coverage should never be assumed. Read CPS warranty FAQs →

Already have a CPS plan?

Keep your purchase receipt and plan details available. Follow the registration and claim instructions supplied with your plan; CPS determines coverage under its terms. Visit CPS for plan support →

Warranty questions

Common questions.

Is a warranty included in PHE Membership?

CPS protection plans are separate from PHE Membership. Membership provides the technology care and service benefits listed on our membership page; it does not automatically enroll equipment in a CPS warranty.

How much does a TV or audio warranty cost?

Ask PHE for pricing for your equipment and the available plan term. We’ll discuss options before you purchase; there is no single price for every TV or audio system.

Can I protect equipment I already own?

Eligibility depends on the equipment, purchase timing and plan requirements. Share your model and purchase details with PHE so we can check available options.

Does every plan include in-home repairs or accidental damage?

No. Service arrangements and covered events depend on the specific CPS plan. Review the plan documents for those details before enrolling.

Coverage, eligibility, pricing, exclusions and service are subject to the applicable CPS plan terms. This page is an overview and does not replace your plan documents.
